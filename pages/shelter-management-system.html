Project Overview

A next-generation web application designed to bridge the gap between homeless pets and loving families. By integrating Artificial Intelligence and QR technology, the platform transforms the adoption process from a manual, time-consuming task into a personalized, interactive experience. The system focuses on three core pillars: Intelligent Matching, Instant Assistance, and Seamless Identification.

Interface Showcase

Key Features

  • 01AI-Powered Pet Matching

    Personalized recommendations, compatibility scoring, favorites, swipe browsing, and pet comparison.

  • 02WAGGO Chatbot

    NLP-powered virtual assistance with multilingual inquiry support.

  • 03QR Code Identification

    Unique pet QR tags with instant access to digital pet profiles.

  • 04Pet Record Management

    Centralized pet profiles, record updates, archiving, and availability tracking.

  • 05Adoption Management

    Online applications, approval workflows, pickup scheduling, and request tracking.

  • 06Shelter Service Requests

    Pet turnover, volunteer registration, and donation request management.

  • 07Donation Management

    Digital donation submissions, payment instructions, receipt verification, and tracking.

  • 08Dashboard and Analytics

    Operational summaries, data visualization, and request monitoring.

  • 09Reports and Announcements

    Account creation, role-based access, profile management, and activity logs.

  • 10User and Staff Management

    Report generation, PDF/Excel exports, and announcement publishing.
